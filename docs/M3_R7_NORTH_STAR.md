## Mission

NOMOGENESIS is the proposed foundation of programmable
economic coordination infrastructure for autonomous agents.

The first implemented market is joint recovery of failed
dependencies across independently financed jobs.

The goal is NOT another generic escrow, crowdfunding
application, AI wrapper or workflow dashboard.

The goal is to demonstrate when individually infeasible
economic commitments can become collectively executable,
bonded and objectively settleable.

## Competition target

Target: 98/100 on our INTERNAL evidence-readiness rubric.

This is a development aspiration, not a judge score,
winning probability or claim of economic superiority.

Priority NEAR alignment:
1. AI Swarms — economically coordinated autonomous agents.
2. Agent Economy — bounded spending and service contracting.
3. Open Innovation — reusable coordination mechanism.

Potential NEAR Intents integration is future research.
No existing Intents integration is claimed.

Social reach is not an optimization objective.
A compliant, concise public demonstration is sufficient
for our submission workflow.

## Four mandatory evidence pillars

### P1 — Economic emergence

Compare the SAME scenarios across:
- independently financed recovery;
- NOMOGENESIS joint rescue;
- full standby reservation;
- reactive collective spot search.

Measure:
- recovery feasibility;
- saved work / deadline completion;
- modelled incremental net value;
- cash cost;
- temporarily locked capital;
- negative cases and alternatives that win.

No cherry-picked aggregate superiority claim.

### P2 — Autonomous actors

Independently deciding buyer and provider policies.
Role-separated signing and auditable decisions.
No claim that deterministic agents are LLMs.
No claim of decentralization from multiple accounts
controlled by one operator.

### P3 — Competitive clearing

Multiple admissible and inadmissible competing offers.
Cheapest valid complete pair must win.
Show insufficient bonds, shared capacity locks,
conflicting slots and deadline rejection.

### P4 — Adversarial economic behavior

Demonstrate caps, ownership, replay protection,
malformed proofs, defaults and accounting conservation.
Document strategic attacks that remain unresolved.

## R7-01 — Economic Emergence Benchmark

First deliverable: an offline deterministic comparison
of joint recovery against competing strategies.

No external state, blockchain writes or randomized
narrative results.

A positive test MUST demonstrate joint-funding benefit.
A negative test MUST demonstrate standby superiority.
Another negative test MUST demonstrate spot superiority.
An infeasible case MUST reject all available strategies.

The initial benchmark is an exploratory model.
It is NOT empirical proof of general market advantage.
